Run it on your own video

The heavy stages (frame extraction, COLMAP) run on your own Mac. Install the helper once, then this page drives it:

brew install ffmpeg uv
uvx --from git+https://github.com/danielhkuo/aperture-tilt-shift sa gui

Keep that terminal open and . Chrome may ask permission to reach a local device. In Safari open 127.0.0.1:8549 directly. Source & capture notes.
